# CLAUDE.md — apps/user (vehicle-owner app)

See the root `CLAUDE.md` for monorepo commands and repo-wide rules (shared package, theming, Sinhala UI, maps key).

## Architecture

- `src/app/_layout.tsx` renders `src/App.tsx`; there is no router navigation (`src/app/index.tsx` returns null). `App.tsx` owns the tab state (`BottomNav`: home / bids / activity / mart / profile), renders one `Header` above every tab (like the garage, parts and technician apps), and opens full-screen flows as `<Modal>`s: SOS (`SOSMapPickerScreen` → `SOSFlowScreen`), post a job (`PostJobScreen`), category browse (`ServiceBrowseScreen`). The direct-booking form (`DirectBookingSheet`) is opened from Home, a category's garage list, or "book again" in Activity.
- Provider order (outer → inner): `ThemeProvider` → `NoticeProvider` → `WorkshopProvider` → `VehiclesProvider` → `BookingsProvider` → `AppShell` → `LocationProvider` → `MartProvider` → `BidsProvider`. `AppShell` reads the theme so a switch re-renders everything without remounting.
- Stores in `src/context`: `NoticeContext` (toasts; `Toast` and the `Sheet` wrapper show them, also inside each modal), `LocationContext` (expo-location GPS + reverse geocode), `VehiclesContext` (seed vehicles + user-added ones persisted in AsyncStorage), `BidsContext` (posted jobs; simulates garages bidding 6/14/24 s after a post), `BookingsContext` (direct bookings; simulates the garage answering in 5 s: confirms slots between 8:00 and 17:00, otherwise proposes the next 9:00, which the owner accepts or declines — the same rules as the garage app's direct requests), `WorkshopContext` (the workshop steps of every confirmed booking and accepted bid, keyed by booking / job id; simulates the garage receiving the vehicle at the booked time, sending a diagnosis, repairing approved lines, handing over, reworking after a reported problem, scanning the owner's code and answering warranty claims; asking for Recon on jobs posted with Genuine; replying to the owner's review). One seeded direct booking (`db-seed`, brake job) starts with a diagnosis waiting for approval; two past jobs (`HISTORY`, closed records with bills, receipt numbers, one review with a reply, one expired warranty) are the completed-services list — there is no separate history mock.
- Owner-side types are in `src/types.ts` (`DirectBooking`); formatting comes from `src/utils/format.ts` (re-exported from `@ongarage/shared`). Never use `Alert.alert` — tell the owner with `notify()` or a real flow.
- Job cards (Bids) and booking cards (Activity) open `OwnerDetailView` by swiping sideways (shared `SwipeCard`) or tapping the header — the same cross-dissolve detail pattern as the garage app's `JobDetailView`. It follows the live record, so status changes show while it is open, including the workshop record (`WorkshopTracker detailed`).
- `components/workshop`: `WorkshopTracker` (step bar, the owner's next action, the sheets) sits on Activity cards; `ApprovalSheet` (tick lines, new total, or stop for the inspection fee; with `extraId` the same for extra work found mid-repair, where "none" just means the agreed work only), `HandoverSheet` (report + bill, then the `CloseCode`; a closed job moves to the completed list only after the owner dismisses it), `ProblemSheet` (AI `triageComplaint` suggests the topic), `WarrantySheet`, `ReconSheet` (Genuine vs Recon price; approving rewrites the line to Recon at the Recon price), `RatingSheet` (four `RATING_DIMENSIONS` + the technician; the comment goes through AI `moderate` first; only code-closed jobs can be rated), `ReceiptSheet`, `GuaranteeSheet` (OnGarage Guarantee claim on a protected job: eligibility checklist, amount, split; simulated garage response then OnGarage decision). Jobs with a Premier garage start `protected`; the seeded past job `h1` (AutoTech Motors, Premier) is protected. Refer to mock garages by id (e.g. `SPEED_WORKS`), never by array position. `GarageReviewsSheet` (level, dimension averages, reviews with the garage's replies) opens from a `GarageCard`. `GarageCard` reports saving through `onSaveChange` (the app shows a toast; never `Alert`).
- Photos and voice notes on job posts and bookings use `MediaAttachments` (sample photos and a timed, silent recording until native camera / microphone access).
- `SOSFlowScreen` is the 11-step SOS loop (confirm → consent → searching with radius and call-out-fee growth → bids → accepted → tracking → arrived → repairing → completion notice → QR scan with the 6-digit code under it (shared `CloseCode`) → rating). `consent` and `repairCompleteNotice` render as overlays on the previous step.
- `ServiceBrowseScreen` has a three-state bottom sheet (peek / collapsed / expanded) driven by a PanResponder; interrupted gestures must still `settle()`, and the peek height is measured from the "Top Rated" row.
- **OnMart tab** (`screens/MartScreen`, `context/MartContext`, `components/mart`): segments Shops / My requests / Open wall / Orders. Shops: nearby shops (shared `MART_SHOPS` through `shopsNear`) with sort chips, 10 km / 50 km / nationwide chips, a part search (live-stock shops show availability, the rest are asked), service kits (`KitSheet`) and `ShopSheet`. `EnquirySheet` (shared `PartBriefForm` + `MediaAttachments`, audience shops / wall / both, AI `checkOffPlatform` and `moderate` before sending) creates a `PartEnquiry`; `MartContext` simulates shops answering from the shared stock (a reply delay that follows each shop's usual speed; the wall also gets a late "now listed" offer) and holds enquiries, offers, purchases and shop threads. `EnquiryCard` lists offers cheapest first with a price tag, a fitment note and, for a garage's recommended shops, the referral disclosure. `ReserveSheet` leads to `OrdersList` (purchase code via `PurchaseCode`, `CounterSheet` inspection checklist then buy or reject, warranty card, return, rating; also shown in Activity) and `ThreadSheet` (quick questions, photo, call; off-app text is held back). `JobPartsToBuy` shows parts a garage asked the owner to buy for a booked job (`DiagnosisLine.source === 'owner'`); asking prefills the enquiry with the job's `jobRef`, and `buy()` tells `WorkshopContext.markPartBought` so the simulated garage can hand over. The seeded brake job's extra work asks the owner to buy the discs (recommended: Galle Auto Parts, Karapitiya Recon Centre). The tab icon is hand-drawn (`components/MartNavIcon`) because `NavIcons.tsx` is generated.
- **OnMart landing** (`components/mart/MartHeader` band, then the Shops segment of `MartScreen`): a bigger search bar with a vehicle fit line and two gradient action cards that fold away on scroll; then, revealed one after another (`Reveal`): the round part strip (`PartStrip`, `PART_GROUPS`, each opens a search), paid banners (`MartBannerCarousel`: auto-paging, labelled "දැන්වීම"), offer tiles (Home's `OfferTiles`, now generic), service kits (`KitCards`), deal cards (`DealsSection` from `liveDeals`: −% badge, countdown, was / now price; "all" shows a grid), and shop rows (`ShopRow` / `ShopTile`: the paid Featured row from `featuredShops(SPONSORED_SHOP_IDS)`, then `organicRows`: fast, top rated, nearest, newly joined, live stock). Covers are gradients with initials until shops have photos. Targets (`MartTarget`) open a shop, a search, a kit, the wall or scroll to the deals. While searching, the landing hides and the familiar results show. Rules live in the shared `marketplace/deals.ts`; fixtures in `constants/martPromos.ts`.
- Mock data lives in `src/constants/mockData.ts`; the service categories come from `@ongarage/shared` (`SERVICE_CATEGORIES`).
- Home (`components/home`, `constants/home.ts`): category photos, garage covers and banners are bundled in `assets/home` (Unsplash / Pexels licence) so Home works offline. Paid placements (`SPONSORED_GARAGE_IDS`, `PROMO_BANNERS`) are always labelled "දැන්වීම" and only appear in the banners and the Featured row; the other rows are organic (newly joined, popular by rating × reviews, nearest), so ads never buy ranking.
- Layout (PickMe-inspired): bottom tabs are English (Home, Bids, Activity, OnMart, Account). `App.tsx` shows the sky-blue greeting `Header` on Home only; Bids and Activity get a `TitleBand` in the same colour; Account has no header (full screen). The tab's screen sits in a rounded sheet overlapping the band (screens have transparent backgrounds; the sheet supplies white / body colour). Bids puts a full-screen map behind a `ThreeStateSheet` (peek / collapsed / expanded; peek shows received bids as round photos with price badges; the tab bar floats 72px high, so pass `bottomInset`). The sheet's handle supports both tap and drag; keep `userSelect: none` on draggable surfaces on the web.
- `ProfileContext` stores the owner's details (AsyncStorage `ongarage.owner.profile`); `EditProfileScreen` edits them with validation; the 7-step completion bar lives only on the edit page.

## Tab-bar icons

The four tab icons are the designer's artwork in `assets/updated_icons/*.svg`. `scripts/build-nav-icons.cjs` converts them to `src/components/NavIcons.tsx` (generated — don't hand-edit): navy → `ink`, sky blue → `accent`, so they follow the theme and the active/idle state. To change an icon, replace the SVG and run `node apps/user/scripts/build-nav-icons.cjs` from the repo root. `BottomNav` takes an optional `icon` component per item; both colours come from the shared `navIconTones`. The garage app's tab icons (`apps/garage/src/components/NavIcons.tsx`) are hand-drawn in the same two-tone style; the parts and technician apps still pass a `path`.
